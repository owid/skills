The user asked how many children died in Somalia in 2024 according to Our World in Data.

PASS if the reply gives a number of under-five (child) deaths for Somalia and states the year that number is for. If the data includes 2024, giving the 2024 figure is correct. If the latest available year is earlier than 2024, the reply must say so and give that year's figure instead of extrapolating.

FAIL if the reply gives no count at all (only a rate such as deaths per 1,000 births, without explaining that no count is available), or presents a figure without saying which year it refers to, or invents a 2024 number while stating the data ends earlier.
