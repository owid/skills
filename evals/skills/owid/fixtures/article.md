# Progress against child mortality

Child mortality has fallen dramatically over the past three decades. In 1990,
around 12.8 million children died before their fifth birthday. By 2022 the
number had fallen to under 5 million.

Somalia has the highest child mortality rate in the world: one in five children
born there dies before the age of five.

Sub-Saharan Africa still accounts for more than half of all child deaths
worldwide, even though it is home to about 15% of the world's children.

Malaria remains the leading cause of death among children under five globally.
